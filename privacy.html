隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 7 日　运营者：孙俊

游戏记录与在线功能

设备会保存牌局进度、偏好、个人资料和成就。使用在线排行榜时，我们处理玩家标识、昵称、成绩和排名所需的牌局记录。其他玩家可以看到你的展示昵称和成绩。若启用存档同步，同步内容包括存档内的牌局进度、成就以及你选择填写的个人资料。

你可以选择预设头像或从照片中选取头像。启用公开照片头像后，所选头像会上传供其他玩家查看；你可以更换头像或关闭公开。扫描挑战二维码时，二维码在设备上识别。

身份、钱包与购买

游戏使用玩家、安装及会话标识关联账户、成绩和解题券。使用 Game Center 时，我们处理 Apple 提供的游戏身份标识和验证资料。Apple 负责处理付款。我们使用商品标识、已签名的交易凭证、交易标识、账户关联信息及解题券发放和使用记录，核对付款与退款、更新余额并避免重复发券。

服务提供方与保留期限

提供这些功能涉及 Apple 和承载游戏服务的基础设施提供方。游戏不投放广告，不进行跨 App 跟踪，也不使用另行接收玩家数据的第三方分析服务。

存档、榜单和钱包资料按提供相应功能所需时间保留。购买凭证按核账、退款及适用法律所需时间保留。删除请求处理后，备份中的相应资料随实际备份轮换周期清除。

联系与数据请求

如需查阅、更正个人资料、撤回授权或请求删除，请致信 jsun47@me.com，说明涉及的游戏资料和请求。运营者会核实归属后处理。请勿在邮件中提供 Apple 账户密码、验证码或付款卡号。

Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026 · Operator: 孙俊

Game records and online features

Your device stores game progress, preferences, profile details and achievements. When you use online leaderboards, we process your player identifier, nickname, scores and the game records needed to rank scores. Other players can see your display name and scores. If save syncing is enabled, the synced save includes game progress, achievements and the profile details you choose to enter.

You may choose a preset avatar or select a photo. If you enable a public photo avatar, the selected image is uploaded for other players to see. You can replace it or turn off public display. Challenge QR codes are read on your device.

Identity, wallet and purchases

We use player, installation and session identifiers to associate your account, scores and solution tickets. If you use Game Center, we process the game identity and verification information supplied by Apple. Apple handles payment. We use product and transaction identifiers, signed transaction records, account references and ticket grants and usage to verify purchases and refunds, update your balance and prevent duplicate grants.

Service providers and retention

These features use Apple services and infrastructure providers that host the game services. The game has no advertising or cross-app tracking and uses no separate analytics service that receives player data.

We retain saves, leaderboard records and wallet records for as long as needed to provide those features. Purchase records are kept as needed for reconciliation, refunds and applicable legal requirements. After a deletion request is processed, corresponding backup copies expire through the normal backup rotation.

Contact and data requests

To access or correct your data, withdraw permission or request deletion, email jsun47@me.com and identify the relevant game records and your request. The operator will verify ownership before processing it. Please keep Apple Account passwords, verification codes and payment card numbers out of your email.
