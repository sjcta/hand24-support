游戏记录与在线功能

设备会保存牌局进度、偏好、个人资料和成就。iOS 1.0（构建版本 53）的在线排行榜展示系统生成的昵称、预设头像和成绩。你在此版本编辑的昵称、选取的照片和取得的成绩保存在本机。

在提供资料同步或排行榜上传的网页版和旧版本中，服务会处理玩家标识、昵称、成绩，以及计算排名所需的牌局记录。榜上可以显示玩家选用的昵称和成绩。若开启公开照片头像，所选照片会上传供其他玩家查看；你可以更换头像或关闭公开。若启用存档同步，云端存档包括牌局进度、成就及你选择填写的个人资料。

扫描挑战二维码时，二维码在设备上识别。

身份、钱包与购买

游戏使用玩家、安装及会话标识关联账户、成绩和解题券。使用 Game Center 时，我们处理 Apple 提供的游戏身份标识和验证资料。Apple 负责处理付款。我们使用商品标识、已签名的交易凭证、交易标识、账户关联信息及解题券发放和使用记录，核对付款与退款、更新余额并避免重复发券。

服务提供方与保留期限

提供这些功能涉及 Apple 和承载游戏服务的基础设施提供方。游戏不投放广告，不进行跨 App 跟踪，也不使用另行接收玩家数据的第三方分析服务。

存档、榜单和钱包资料按提供相应功能所需时间保留。购买凭证按核账、退款及适用法律所需时间保留。删除请求处理后，备份中的相应资料随实际备份轮换周期清除。

联系与数据请求

在 iOS 1.0（构建版本 53），使用与游戏账户关联的 Game Center 玩家登录，打开「我的 → 账户与绑定 → 删除 HAND²⁴ 账户」，阅读说明后选择「永久删除」。如果画面提示服务器尚未确认，可以联网后用原 Game Center 玩家重试。

验证通过后，服务器删除该游戏账户、Game Center 绑定、登录会话、云存档和剩余解题券。本机收到确认后会清除该账户的资料、牌局、成就和钱包缓存。其他安装此版本且仍选中被删账户的设备，会在同一 Game Center 玩家联网重新验证并收到删除确认后清理该账户的缓存。删除无法撤销，范围限于所选 HAND²⁴ 账户。Apple 账户、Game Center 账户以及独立的游客或网页资料分别管理。

为核账、处理退款及防止重复发券，服务保留已解除 Game Center 绑定的必要交易记录。为防止旧存档恢复已删除的账户、识别重复删除请求，服务也保留经散列处理的标识摘要。

如需查阅、更正个人资料、撤回授权，或在网页版和旧版本中请求删除，请致信 jsun47@me.com，说明涉及的游戏资料和请求。运营者会核实归属后处理。请勿在邮件中提供 Apple 账户密码、验证码或付款卡号。

Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026 · Operator: 孙俊

Game records and online features

Your device stores game progress, preferences, profile details and achievements. In iOS 1.0 (build 53), the online leaderboard shows system-generated names, preset avatars and scores. The name you edit, the photo you choose and the scores you earn in this version are stored on your device.

On the web and in earlier versions that offer profile syncing or score uploads, the service processes player identifiers, nicknames, scores and the game records needed to calculate rankings. Other players may see player-selected names and scores. If you enable a public photo avatar, the selected image is uploaded for other players to see. You can change the image or turn off public display. If you enable save syncing, the cloud save includes your progress, achievements and the profile details you choose to enter.

Challenge QR codes are read on your device.

Identity, wallet and purchases

We use player, installation and session identifiers to associate your account, scores and solution tickets. If you use Game Center, we process the game identity and verification information supplied by Apple. Apple handles payment. We use product and transaction identifiers, signed transaction records, account references and ticket grants and usage to verify purchases and refunds, update your balance and prevent duplicate grants.

Service providers and retention

These features use Apple services and infrastructure providers that host the game services. The game has no advertising or cross-app tracking and uses no separate analytics service that receives player data.

We retain saves, leaderboard records and wallet records for as long as needed to provide those features. Purchase records are kept as needed for reconciliation, refunds and applicable legal requirements. After a deletion request is processed, corresponding backup copies expire through the normal backup rotation.

Contact and data requests

In iOS 1.0 (build 53), sign in as the Game Center player linked to your game account. Open Profile → Account and sign-in → Delete HAND²⁴ account. Read the confirmation and choose Delete permanently. If the app is waiting for server confirmation, retry online using the same Game Center player.

After verifying the request, the server deletes the game account, its Game Center link, sign-in sessions, cloud save and remaining solution tickets. Your device removes the account’s profile, game records, achievements and cached wallet when it receives confirmation. On another device running this version with the deleted account still selected, the app clears its cached data after the same Game Center player verifies their identity online and the server confirms deletion. Deletion is permanent and applies to the selected HAND²⁴ account. Apple Accounts, Game Center accounts and independent guest or web profiles are managed separately.

We keep the transaction records needed for reconciliation, refunds and preventing duplicate grants, with the Game Center link removed. We also keep hashed identifiers to prevent old saves from restoring deleted accounts and to recognize repeated deletion requests.

To access or correct your data, withdraw permission or request deletion on the web or in an earlier version, email jsun47@me.com and identify the relevant game records and your request. The operator will verify ownership before processing it. Please keep Apple Account passwords, verification codes and payment card numbers out of your email.
